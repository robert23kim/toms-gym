# 2026-10-31: The 100 Club (consistency challenge)

## Asked
A challenge about consistency rather than volume: 100 "lifts" a week (situps, pushups, squats or planks); hit it and stay in, with a higher bar next week; a points system with a bonus for the week's leader in a lift type. Implement, deploy and verify autonomously; Tom tests it himself.

## Decisions
- Tom's (from the interview, recorded in the design doc `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-100-club-design.md`): the four lift types, Mon-Sun weeks starting 2026-11-02 for 8 weeks, quota 100 then +25 per met week and reset on a miss, the points formula, the ranking tiebreaks.
- Builder's: no new table (metadata `mode`/`rules`, computed on read), plank 10 s = 1 lift, America/New_York week boundaries, warm-up before week 1, live current week with provisional type leaders, ties share the bonus, the admin `as_of` preview, the "Consistency" label.
- Open for Tom: a minimum amount before a type bonus counts (QA-5).
