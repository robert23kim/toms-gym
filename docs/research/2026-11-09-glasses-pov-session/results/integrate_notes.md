## What track_v3.py is
`S/track_v3.py <stem> [--device mps|cpu] [--audit-log PATH]`. It imports `experiments/motion-only/run.py` unchanged (importlib), points its output folders at `S/work/v3/` (MO.X, MO.WORK) and calls its `main()` with `--any-stem`. That run.py already composes, by import from L2 `track_v2.py`: composition edge lane (`CM.run`, ball points injected via `CM.ball_stage`), `clean_series`, `pin_row_estimate` (geometric) + `pin_motion` override, `deck_decoy`, `add_depth`, `compute_events`, `draw_overlay_v2`. Nothing is re-implemented. track_v3 then writes `S/results/tracks/<stem>.json` (the loop-2 track format as produced, plus `method: track_v3`, `stages` {ball, lane, pin_row, events, path, pick}, `pick_flag`, `meta.track_v3` with sha256 prefixes of run.py and track_v3.py and the runtime local-module list) and `S/overlays/final__<stem>.jpg` (the track_v2 overlay plus a 3-line strip: PARTIAL pick flag, pin-row frame + source, entry vs final_board). `S/overlays/picture__<stem>.jpg` (20/40/55 ft crops) is copied for J6.
Reproduction check: the smoke run on 104946 gave entry 6.18, pin row f447 geometric, 80 points {sam 16, residual 63, coast 1}, identical to the motion-only dev table.
